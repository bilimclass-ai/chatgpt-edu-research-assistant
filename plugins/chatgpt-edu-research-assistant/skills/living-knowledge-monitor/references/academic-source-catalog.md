# Academic source catalog

Reviewed: 2026-09-08. Treat this as a maintained routing catalog, not proof that every listed service is accessible, exhaustive, or appropriate for every topic.

## Evidence roles

- **Index:** locates and cross-checks scholarly records; verify important claims on a publisher, DOI, repository, or official record.
- **Discovery:** surfaces leads, announcements, or people; never use it alone to confirm deadlines, funding, eligibility, peer-review status, affiliation, or reputation.
- **Verification:** an official publisher, organizer, funder, institution, regulator, or program page that can support operational claims.
- **Integrity:** helps assess journal legitimacy, corrections, retractions, or publication status; it does not replace reading the article and journal policies.

## Per-run source plan

Build a source plan before searching:

1. Check every accessible source marked `always_review` in the profile.
2. Check all enabled preferred sources.
3. For each enabled report category, check the relevant category sources below.
4. Add discipline and geographic sources matching the profile; Kazakhstan-focused monitors must include the Kazakhstan sources.
5. Rotate optional broad sources when checking all of them would add duplication without meaningful coverage.

Save `source-coverage/YYYY-MM-DD.json` with: source name, URL, evidence role, category, checked time, query or filter summary, result count before screening when known, included count, and status `checked|no relevant update|inaccessible|subscription unavailable|blocked|skipped with reason`.

Do not claim that a source was reviewed when only a search-engine snippet was seen. If a platform requires login, subscription, CAPTCHA, or blocks automated access, record the limitation and continue with accessible alternatives. Never bypass access controls or prohibited automation. Prefer official RSS feeds, APIs, saved searches, and alerts when available, but do not subscribe, log in, or create alerts without authorization.

## 1. Cross-disciplinary scholarly indexes

Use a complementary mix rather than assuming any single index is complete.

- [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) — index; open works, authors, institutions, sources, topics, and funders.
- [Crossref Metadata Search](https://search.crossref.org/) — index; DOI and publication metadata verification.
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) — discovery/index; broad coverage, but verify records elsewhere and do not automate prohibited scraping.
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/) — discovery/index; papers, authors, and citation connections.
- [Dimensions](https://app.dimensions.ai/discover/publication) — index; publications, grants, patents, datasets, and clinical trials; access level varies.
- [The Lens](https://www.lens.org/) — index; scholarly works and patents; account or usage limits may apply.
- [Scopus](https://www.scopus.com/) — subscription index; use when institutional access exists.
- [Web of Science](https://www.webofscience.com/) — subscription index; use when institutional access exists.
- [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/) — index/integrity; open-access journals and articles.
- [CORE](https://core.ac.uk/search) — index; open-access repository content.
- [BASE](https://www.base-search.net/) — index; academic web resources from repositories.

## 2. Discipline databases and libraries

Enable sources that match the research area.

### Education and social sciences

- [ERIC](https://eric.ed.gov/) — index; education research and policy literature.
- [Education Research Complete](https://www.ebsco.com/products/research-databases/education-research-complete) — subscription index.
- [SSRN](https://www.ssrn.com/) — repository/discovery; working papers and preprints, especially social sciences.
- [ProQuest Dissertations & Theses Global](https://about.proquest.com/en/products-services/pqdtglobal/) — subscription index; theses and dissertations.

### Linguistics, language, and humanities

- [MLA International Bibliography](https://www.mla.org/Publications/MLA-International-Bibliography) — subscription index; language, literature, linguistics, and related humanities.
- [Linguistics and Language Behavior Abstracts](https://about.proquest.com/en/products-services/llba-set-c/) — subscription index.
- [The LINGUIST List](https://linguistlist.org/) — discovery/community; publications, calls, conferences, jobs, and language resources.
- [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/) — repository/index; computational linguistics and language technology.

### Computing and AI

- [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/) — publisher/index; engineering, computing, conference, and standards literature.
- [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/) — publisher/index; computing publications and proceedings.
- [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/) — preprint repository; label material as not necessarily peer reviewed.
- [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/) — venue/submission environment; reviews and publication status vary by venue.

### Health and life sciences

- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) — index; biomedical literature.
- [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/) — index/repository; life-science publications, preprints, grants, and full text where available.
- [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/) — official registry; study registration and results, not proof of peer-reviewed findings.

## 3. Preprints, repositories, data, and protocols

- [OSF](https://osf.io/preprints/) — preprints and open research projects.
- [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/) — repository; publications, data, software, and other research outputs.
- [HAL](https://hal.science/) — open multidisciplinary repository.
- [Research Square](https://www.researchsquare.com/) — preprint platform.
- [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org/) and [medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org/) — preprints; label review status and health-evidence limitations.
- [Protocol.io](https://www.protocols.io/) — research protocols and methods; check licensing and access.
- [Open Science Framework Registries](https://osf.io/registries) — registrations and preregistrations.
- [DataCite Commons](https://commons.datacite.org/) — index; DOI-linked datasets, software, works, people, and organizations.

## 4. Scientific conferences and calls for papers

Use discovery platforms to find candidates, then verify on the organizer or conference website. Apply [Think. Check. Attend.](https://thinkcheckattend.org/) when venue legitimacy is uncertain.

### Association and venue sources

- [IEEE Conference Search](https://events.ieee.org/search-ieee-conferences/) — association conference directory.
- [ACM Conference Events](https://www.acm.org/conferences/conference-events) — association events and conferences.
- [LINGUIST List conference calls](https://linguistlist.org/conf/search/?type=Call) — language and linguistics calls.
- [ACL Events](https://www.aclweb.org/portal/events) — computational linguistics events.
- [OpenReview active venues](https://openreview.net/) — calls and submission deadlines for participating venues.
- [EasyChair Smart CFP](https://easychair.org/cfp/) — discovery/submission environment; verify on the event's official page.

### Broad discovery sources

- [WikiCFP](https://www.wikicfp.com/cfp/) — community-maintained discovery only.
- [Conference Alerts](https://conferencealerts.com/) — discovery only.

For each included conference, open the official call and verify organizer, venue or format, event dates, call opening, submission deadline and timezone, acceptance notification, fees, travel grants, waivers, funding, and eligibility. A submission-platform page may verify its own deadline but does not automatically verify conference reputation or funding.

Always verify citizenship, residence, career stage, host, institutional nomination, language, mobility, and co-funding requirements. Never infer eligibility from nationality or job title alone.

- [jobs.ac.uk](https://www.jobs.ac.uk/) — discovery; verify with the employer.
- [Nature Careers](https://www.nature.com/naturecareers/jobs/) — academic and research jobs.

- [HigherEdJobs](https://www.higheredjobs.com/) — higher-education job discovery.

## 7. Researchers, laboratories, and academic communities

- [ORCID Registry](https://orcid.org/) — stable researcher identifiers and self-managed records.
- [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) — author, institution, and works discovery.
- [VIVO](https://vivoweb.org/) and institutional VIVO portals — expert and research-network discovery.
- [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/) — community/discovery only; do not use alone to verify affiliation or peer-review status.
- [Academia.edu](https://www.academia.edu/) — community/discovery only.
- [Humanities Commons](https://hcommons.org/) — humanities community and open scholarship.
- [H-Net](https://networks.h-net.org/) — humanities and social-science networks and announcements.
- Learned societies, university staff directories, department pages, lab sites, project consortia, and conference program committees relevant to the profile.

Verify a collaborator with a current institutional page plus an ORCID or representative recent work when possible. Community activity is a signal of fit, not proof of affiliation, reputation, availability, or willingness to collaborate.

## 8. Journal special issues, collections, and series announcements

- [Springer Nature publishing and Collections route](https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/publish-an-article) — publisher route to journal calls and topical collections.
- [Wiley Special Issues](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/publish/article/editor-resources/special-issues/) — publisher guidance and journal calls.
- [Taylor & Francis author portal](https://www.tandfonline.com/page/authors/journal) — routes to current calls for papers by subject.
- [Elsevier special issues](https://www.elsevier.com/en-gb/publishing/publish-in-a-journal/journal-selection/special-issues) — publisher calls and journal-specific special issues.
- [Frontiers Research Topics](https://www.frontiersin.org/research-topics) — open article collections and deadlines.
- [Emerald calls for papers](https://www.emeraldgrouppublishing.com/calls-for-papers) — journal and book calls.
- Individual journal announcement pages, learned-society newsletters, journal RSS feeds, and publisher email alerts explicitly selected by the user.

For every included call, verify journal title, publisher, scope, article types, submission deadline, guest editors, submission route, peer-review statement, indexing evidence, open-access model, and article-processing charge or waiver. Do not equate publisher reputation, special-issue status, or claimed indexing with suitability. Check the journal against the profile's accepted indexes and the official Kazakhstan quality list when applicable.

## 9. Publication integrity and journal due diligence

- [Crossmark](https://www.crossref.org/services/crossmark/) — current status, corrections, and updates when implemented by the publisher.
- [Retraction Watch Database](https://retractiondatabase.org/) — retraction discovery; confirm details against publisher/Crossref records when possible.
- [COPE](https://publicationethics.org/) — publication-ethics guidance and member search.
- [Think. Check. Submit.](https://thinkchecksubmit.org/) — journal and publisher due-diligence checklist.
- [DOAJ](https://doaj.org/) — open-access journal listing and metadata.
- Official Scopus, Web of Science, and Kazakhstan quality-list records when the user's acceptance criteria require them.

Do not label a journal “predatory” from a single list or missing index. Report the specific verified risk signals, unknowns, fees, and indexing evidence.

## 10. Policies, standards, and higher-education environments

Use official pages from the relevant ministry, regulator, accreditor, standards body, professional association, funder, and institution. Common global sources include:

- [UNESCO Higher Education](https://www.unesco.org/en/higher-education)
- [OECD Education](https://www.oecd.org/education/)
- [European Commission Education](https://education.ec.europa.eu/)
- [ISO](https://www.iso.org/standards.html) and discipline-specific standards bodies.

Track announcement date, consultation status, adoption date, effective date, jurisdiction, affected institutions, and the official document—not commentary alone.
