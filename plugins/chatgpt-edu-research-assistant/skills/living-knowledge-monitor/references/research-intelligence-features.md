# Research-intelligence features

Use these rules when the corresponding profile feature is enabled.

## Action labels

Assign exactly one primary label per delivered item:

- `ACT NOW`: a verified deadline or material decision requires action before the next normal review.
- `READ`: strong evidence worth reading, but no immediate thesis insertion or deadline.
- `USE IN THESIS`: directly informs a thesis construct, gap, method, instrument, analysis, or chapter.
- `COLLABORATE`: a verified researcher, laboratory, or institution merits outreach evaluation.
- `WATCH`: credible but incomplete, early, or awaiting a material update.

Do not use urgency labels merely to make the digest look active.

## Evidence-confidence badges

- `A — Verified primary source`: a primary authoritative page directly supports the key claim.
- `B — Official but preliminary`: an official conference abstract, preprint, protocol, consultation, or announcement lacks mature outcome evidence.
- `C — Incomplete verification`: a credible source has a material field missing or inaccessible; state the gap.
- `D — Watchlist only`: a weak or indirect signal; never present it as an established finding or open opportunity.

Confidence measures evidence strength, not whether a result is positive.

## Thesis Evidence Matrix

Map each relevant item to thesis chapter; construct or variable; research question or hypothesis; method or instrument; population/context; expected contribution; proposed use; limitation or transfer risk. Label proposed use as the monitor's inference. Use `Not yet mapped` instead of forcing a fit.

## Eligibility pre-check

Use one status: `probably eligible`, `conditionally eligible`, `not eligible`, `unknown`, or `not applicable`. List satisfied criteria, unmet criteria, missing facts, and the official source. Never guarantee the decision of a funder, host, immigration authority, or conference. Use `unknown` when the call year, timezone, funding, or open status cannot be confirmed.

## Conference pipeline

Use user-managed stages: `Discovered`, `Eligibility check`, `Abstract idea`, `Drafting`, `Supervisor review`, `Submitted`, `Accepted`, `Funding requested`, `Funded`, `Declined`, `Not pursuing`. Suggest a stage change, but preserve the current stage until the user or an authorized system confirms it.

## Collaboration dossier

Record current role and institution, stable identifier, shared topics, complementary expertise, representative works, recent relevant activity, collaboration hypothesis, proposed first joint output, verified public contact route, outreach status, and last verification date. Do not store private contact details or infer availability, interest, or endorsement.

## Contradiction tracker

Create a contradiction record only when sources address comparable constructs or outcomes. State the relationship as `supports`, `partly supports`, `contradicts`, or `not directly comparable`; then compare population, language, geography, intervention, dosage, instrument, outcome definition, design strength, and publication status. Do not manufacture disagreement from different research questions.

## Instrument library

Record instrument name, construct, task type, population, language, scoring dimensions, reliability/validity evidence, source, availability, adaptation requirements, permission/licence, and proposed thesis use. Use `available`, `adaptable`, `permission required`, `not provided`, or `unknown`. Never reconstruct a proprietary or unavailable instrument from a description.

## Monthly synthesis

Aggregate the month's delivered records and run audits. Report trend direction, strongest evidence, unresolved contradictions, thesis-gap changes, methods/instruments, collaboration movement, opportunity outcomes, and proposed search-profile adjustments. Separate new urgent evidence from synthesis of already delivered material.

## Feedback personalization

Accept `Useful`, `Not useful`, `Already known`, `Too broad`, and `Follow up`, plus optional notes. Use feedback to rerank similar items, sources, methods, and regions. Never silently turn feedback into a hard exclusion. Show proposed profile changes and obtain approval before changing keywords, exclusions, geography, cadence, or sources.
