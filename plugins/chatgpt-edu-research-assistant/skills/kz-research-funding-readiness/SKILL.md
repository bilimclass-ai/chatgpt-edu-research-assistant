# Kazakhstan Research Funding Readiness

Help a professor or institutional research office produce an evidence-backed application and fix avoidable rejection risks. Do not promise selection or describe an internal audit as an official decision. Work in the user's language; produce application text in the language(s) specified by the competition. Preserve Russian or Kazakh legal terminology when translating an explanation.

## Establish the applicable requirements

Read [references/source-map.md](references/source-map.md). The source map distinguishes six supplied PDFs, inspected 2026 amendments and unresolved later changes. Its review date is 7 September 2026, not a perpetual currency certificate. Resolve the Law 321-VIII and NNS V2600039378 gaps before affected current decisions; do not let a dated mirror header establish currency. Retrieve both applicable regulations, amendments and effective dates, the specific competition announcement, approved competition documentation, application form and relevant appendices. Record access date and edition. For a historical application, use the edition legally applicable to that call. An upload can support an edition-specific audit without proving that it is the latest law.

Read [references/funding-rules.md](references/funding-rules.md) for route gates, partner exceptions, staff/publication evidence and budget checks. Identify the actual funding route before evaluating the applicant. Distinguish an individual principal investigator from the applicant entity, and an ordinary research grant from programme-targeted funding, commercialization, base funding and the special institutional fundamental-research route. Do not infer that every NII qualifies for the latter, or that professor status proves eligibility. Follow the relevant chapter and call, rather than applying one route's conditions to all applicants.

If full sources are inaccessible, continue with a clearly provisional gap analysis and ask for the missing document or pages. Do not reconstruct legal requirements from isolated search snippets. A fully retrieved official document body must still be checked for completeness, provenance and effective dates. Do not issue a final eligibility verdict, numerical threshold decision or submission-ready label while a material rule is unverified. Resolve apparent conflicts by checking authority, dates and applicability; do not silently let a call override law or combine obsolete and current provisions.

## Intake and requirement matrix

Extract what is already provided, then request only missing inputs that change the assessment:
- Call name, year, funding route, priority and specialized direction, deadline and timezone, project duration and funding limits.
- Applicant identity and qualifying status; PI and team roles, employment or affiliation evidence, relevant outputs, other current/pending projects and signed commitments where required.
- Proposal, annual budget, facilities/data access, partner commitments, ethics documentation where applicable, required outputs, application languages and current submission/correction status.

Use [assets/readiness-audit.md](assets/readiness-audit.md) as an adaptable working record. Assign a rule ID to each applicable requirement. Record the exact source clause/appendix, edition, evidence location, status, consequence, corrective action and responsible person. Label entries **binding rule**, **call condition**, **recommendation**, or **unresolved interpretation**. Use **met / not met / unknown / not applicable**, explaining non-applicability. Never treat missing evidence as proof of a violation or as compliance.

For the applicable appendix 5 form, verify the minimum 70% detailed planned staff and ten core-team publications in aggregate; these are not a 70% salary rule or ten publications per person. Verify discipline-specific applied-research partner exceptions before requesting a contribution. Check the applicant entity and PI separately. Apply publication windows, degrees, database metrics, team limits, accreditation and other thresholds only when supported by the applicable applicant rules. In particular, do not import the GNTE rules' qualifications for reviewers into PI eligibility. Verify bibliographic identities and the required metric/date; do not substitute journal impact factor, CiteScore percentile, quartile and author h-index for one another.

Check scope overlap using objectives, tasks, methods, deliverables, datasets, dates and funding sources. Compare relevant funded and concurrently submitted work. Explain substantiated differences; do not recommend renaming the same project to conceal duplication. Identify unresolved overlaps for institutional review.

## Budget and institutional evidence

Use the form's required units (including thousands of KZT), price evidence, annual categories and legal-entity restriction on equipment/software. The supplied council/monitor travel order does not set applicant travel rates. Create a budget table with cost category, work package, year, quantity, unit price, currency, conversion basis/date if relevant, taxes/mandatory charges, total, funding source and justification evidence. Recalculate line, annual and project totals. Reconcile them with every narrative and application form. Do not invent prices, tax rates, caps or co-financing percentages.

For each material expense, show which activity and output require it, why the quantity is reasonable, how the price was evidenced, and which verified rule permits it. Check for duplicate personnel effort, overlapping purchases, unsupported travel and implausible procurement lead times. Distinguish committed funding from intended funding and cash from in-kind support if the call recognizes either. Separate questions for the institution's finance office from confirmed errors.

For university teams, organize evidence across the research office, PI, finance and authorized signatory. For NII teams, additionally investigate institutional-route eligibility if that route is requested. Treat these as coordination suggestions, not statutory organizational structures. Do not manufacture signatures, approval letters, publication records, ethics decisions or partner commitments.

## Submission and corrections

Read [references/application-form.md](references/application-form.md) for appendix 5 structure, word limits, nine tables and evidence mapping. Map the actual form's fields, language and length limits, mandatory attachments, signatures and portal requirements. Verify the call's TRL field and relevant methodology instead of inventing readiness levels. Verify the final exported files and portal entries against each other; an uploaded draft is not a submitted application. Prepare an attachment manifest and a final list of unresolved blockers.

For a returned application or challenged NNS decision, read [references/appeals-and-corrections.md](references/appeals-and-corrections.md) and the actual notice. GNTE-171 makes the correction period three working days from the next working day after referral; the appeal period has a different trigger and wording. Apply the relevant edition and working-day calendar. For a returned application, read the applicable correction provision. Record its triggering date/time, working versus calendar days, official holidays and portal deadline. Do not infer the correction deadline from an old snapshot alone. Map each comment to the changed field/file and recheck for new inconsistencies. Distinguish correction from an appeal; verify any appeal route and deadline separately. Use [assets/appeal-record.md](assets/appeal-record.md) for a requested appeal. Apply the amended Commission jurisdiction and exclusions: a score-only or budget-cut-only complaint is not made admissible by relabeling it procedural.

When explaining selection, read [references/nns-assessment.md](references/nns-assessment.md). Separate scientific GNTE, unscored budget assessment, NNS assessment, combined ranking and the available funding line. The bundled NNS scoring is explicitly edition-specific until the later amendment is reconciled. Provide a reviewable final packet and a submission checklist. Submit, sign or message another person only when the user has authorized that action and the necessary tools are available. Report submission as complete only with an actual receipt/status and timestamp; otherwise say prepared for applicant submission.

## Deliver the useful result

Lead with readiness and the most consequential blockers. Provide the source/edition register, compliance matrix, corrected draft or precise fixes, reconciled budget findings, missing-evidence requests, and submission/correction checklist as needed. Separate **formal readiness**, **scientific competitiveness** and **funding outcome**. A formally complete application is not an award prediction.

When the user also requests scientific drafting, connect each proposed improvement to an actual evaluation criterion and evidence. Use the companion proposal-review skill if available, or perform a clearly labeled qualitative review without assuming it is installed.
