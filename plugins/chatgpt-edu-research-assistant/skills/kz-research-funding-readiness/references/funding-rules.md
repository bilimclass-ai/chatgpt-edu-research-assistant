# Funding-route and application checks

Source IDs and currency limitations: `source-map.md`. These checks describe FUND's supplied edition; unresolved LAW/NNS amendments still gate affected current judgments. Separate source requirements from the practical evidence suggestions below.

## Select the route

| Route | Source anchors | Operational consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary research grant, including fundamental research | FUND §§16–42; LAW art.36 | Evaluate the accredited applicant and PI separately. Use the actual call and FUND appendix 5. A project's fundamental character does not make it institutional funding. |
| Programme-targeted funding (ПЦФ) | FUND §§43–67, especially 53,55–58 | Map the scientific/technical task to the programme, every deliverable and budget. Accredited legal entities may form an industrial-scientific technological consortium (§53); verify status and co-executors. §56 requires detailed financial-economic cost justification. |
| Commercialization | FUND §§68–90; LAW arts.40–50 | Use its own call, applicant form, co-financing, IP and market requirements. Do not transfer ordinary-grant correction rights or partner exceptions automatically. |
| Listed institutional fundamental research | FUND §§91–115, appendices 7–8; LAW art.38 | Establish state/100%-state ownership, approved-list status or inclusion application, field and institutional route. Under §95, applications agreed with the sector body go through the Centre system by 1 February of the preceding year; validate current applicability. Funding duration in §92 is at most five years. |
| Base funding | FUND §§5–15; LAW art.35 | Institutional infrastructure support and list rules; not a professor's ordinary competitive grant. |
| Local executive body's applied-research order | FUND chapter 7, §§116 onward | Retrieve the actual local call and chapter; do not silently use the national-grant workflow. |

## Grant/PCF gates

- FUND §§26,53: the supplied edition covers accredited research subjects and autonomous education organizations. Reconcile affected LAW art.36 with Law 321-VIII before confirming current eligibility. An ordinary university's reputation is not accreditation evidence.
- FUND §§28,55: obtain priority, specialized direction, form, language, qualifications, outputs, finance/partner conditions and bioethics documentation requirements. Evaluate actual bioethics applicability against the research and call; do not assert every discipline is exempt or every project needs identical medical approval.
- In this edition, §28(11) gives a 15–30-calendar-day grant intake window from start of receipt; §55(11) gives 15–45 for PCF. Commercialization §73 still gives 30–45 days from announcement. Use the announced closing timestamp and amendments; these ranges do not calculate an individual's live deadline.
- Applied grants/PCF normally require a private partner under §§29,57. Listed exceptions: social sciences, humanities, atomic energy, national security and defence science. Do not infer an exception from institution type. Verify exact subject classification and call requirements.
- Record the contribution agreement, timing, amount/resources and partner status. §29 allows amount or necessary resources in the grant agreement; check PCF wording and call conditions separately. A planned or in-kind contribution is not automatically accepted cash co-financing. Loss of a partner without an equivalent replacement can lead to termination by NNS decision.

## Appendix 5 evidence checks

See `application-form.md` for section limits and table mapping.

- §5 of the explanatory note (PDF p.56): detailed information for at least 70% of planned staff. Up to 30% additional staff may be specified by position, role, work and recruitment approach. Do not apply the percentage to salary costs or round upward a below-threshold identified share.
- List all PI publications proving the call's qualifications, with the required metrics, identifiers and database links. The same section requires at least 10 publications from the core team in total in the project direction, not 10 per person. Count verified records, avoid duplicate citations, and distinguish this inventory requirement from PI qualification and promised future outputs.
- Appendix 5 §2.3(3) calls for technology-readiness information at application and completion. Use evidence and the applicable TRL methodology. For theoretical/humanities work, seek the call's interpretation rather than inventing a prototype or silently deleting a mandatory field. The source inconsistently says 'programme' in a project form: retain the actual call's field meaning.

## Budget controls

FUND §§25,52 permit directly research-related categories: labour; project travel; services/works; materials; equipment/software for legal entities; publication/patenting/analytical support; room/equipment rental; operating costs; and mandatory charges. Permission of a category does not justify a particular amount.

Appendix 5 §7 (PDF pp.57–59):
- Use thousands of KZT where the form requires it; reconcile the budget with §1.5, annual breakdown and tables 2–7. Do not mix KZT and thousands of KZT.
- Show salary, qualifying leave pay and applicable mandatory charges; the form excludes compensation/incentive payments in that calculation. Verify current labour/tax rules and avoid duplicate inclusion of taxes.
- Provide travel plan and carrier-site price offers for tickets. Justify research purpose, dates and outputs.
- For services, materials/equipment and rental/operating lines, attach at least one price offer and/or price list as specified for tables 5–7; check if the call demands more. Outsourcing needs the provider, type, volume and expected output (§§25,52).
- Individuals may not purchase equipment/software under this form. Researchers who are team members belong in the labour calculation; do not automatically classify foreign team members as outsourced services.
- The uploaded travel cross-reference contains an inconsistent year for Decree 256. Verify the actual applicable legal instrument and current rates instead of copying that error. TRAVEL's council/monitor allowances are not the applicant's budget rule.

GNTE-171 replaces the expert budget-assessment appendix 2. Track year, category, proposed reduction, scientific justification and resulting achievable outputs; the budget expert does not award scientific points. Keep requested, scientifically justified, recommended and awarded amounts distinct.

## Delivery commitments

Build a feasibility check before promising results. Record publication/output commitments and grant acknowledgement requirements in the actual call and contract. FUND appendix 5 expected-results section calls for the project identifier/name and grant funding acknowledgement in project articles. Plan reporting, access/IP, partner contribution and evidence retention from the outset. Do not import monitoring/report scores into application scoring or create a recurring reminder without a user scheduling request.
