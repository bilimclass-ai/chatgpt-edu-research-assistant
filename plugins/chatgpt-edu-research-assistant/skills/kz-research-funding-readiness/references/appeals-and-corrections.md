# Corrections, NNS procedures and appeals

Sources: `source-map.md`, APPEAL-BASE with APPEAL-169, GNTE base with GNTE-171. These are different procedures with different triggering events. Verify the edition and actual portal notice for the event date. Do not let a missing document prevent preparation of a provisional chronology and evidence packet.

## Administrative correction under GNTE §§11–12

Under Order 171/2026, §11 requires the Centre's checklist to identify all remarks. Revised §12 requires the applicant to return the corrected application through the Centre's digital system within three working days **from the next working day after referral for correction**. Rechecking concerns elimination of previously identified remarks; non-elimination leads to return within three working days of receiving the revised application. Do not carry forward the older wording about discovering new inconsistencies as if it were the amended §12.

Obtain the referral timestamp, checklist, working-day calendar and portal closing time. Map each remark to the changed field/file and evidence, then check consistency. Preserve the original and corrected versions plus receipt. The organiser's own three-day processing period is not extra time for the applicant. Do not apply this route automatically to commercialization without its applicable provisions.

## Appeal jurisdiction under Order 423 as amended by 169/2026

Updated §14 covers (1) NNS-member ethics, (2) NNS decisions on intermediate/final research or commercialization reports, and (3) noncompliance with assessment/voting procedures for projects/programmes. This replaces the uploaded older general wording about disagreement with an NNS decision.

The unchanged §15 excludes:
- Applications not admitted to NNS consideration.
- Requests concerning reductions of project/programme funding.
- Challenges concerning NCSTE monitoring results as such.
- Anonymous appeals or submissions without the issue, sufficient grounds and facts.
- Requests to increase/decrease NNS or GNTE scores.

A report decision and the underlying monitoring result are not interchangeable. A substantiated procedural/ethics complaint must state its actual facts and remedy; do not disguise a points-only or budget-only disagreement as a procedural complaint. An excluded Commission route does not establish that no other legal remedy exists.

## Time and destination

| Action | Rule in the inspected text | What to record |
|---|---|---|
| Applicant appeal | Amended §17: Centre receives within 3 working days from the NNS decision shown in the applicant's account at https://is.ncste.kz | Decision timestamp and displayed date, exact account notice, official working-day calendar, portal destination and receipt |
| Centre forwards | New §17-1: 1 calendar day after appeal receipt period closes | Forwarding is institutional processing, not an extension for applicants |
| Working-body check | Amended §18: 1 working day after receipt from Centre | Timeliness/admissibility; amended cross-reference is §17, not the old §16 typo |
| Commission review | Base §31: 5 working days from receipt of appeal materials | Review format and material receipt event |
| Notify decision | Amended §42: extract within 1 working day from decision | Extract, reasons and delivery record |

Do **not** import GNTE correction's explicit 'next working day' wording into appeal §17. When a trigger or counting convention is ambiguous, show the earliest plausible deadline, seek official clarification and preserve the shorter internal target without claiming it is a verified legal deadline. Weekend assumptions alone are insufficient; check holidays and transferred working days. For historical spring 2026 cases, resolve the staggered commencement in amendment point 4; specified digital provisions enter on 12 July 2026.

Base §19 suspends implementation of the challenged decision during Commission consideration. Do not promise automatic suspension merely because a draft or email exists. Verify that the matter is being considered.

## Evidence-first preparation

Use `assets/appeal-record.md`. Capture applicant authority, IRN, call, NNS decision, challenged procedural/ethics provision, chronology, precise conduct, original evidence, material effect and the remedy within Commission powers. Attach the submitted application, review/NNS materials, checklist where relevant, portal records and lawfully available minutes/video. Distinguish facts, inferences and unverified allegations. Request missing records through the proper channel; do not seek private access to reviewers.

NNS-BASE appendix 12 requires objective, independent and confidential conduct. Use current membership and the facts of a relationship to investigate a potential conflict; a name match or affiliation alone is not a concluded ethics violation. Verify the later NNS amendment before invoking a particular procedure as current.

Amended appeal §35 requires recording and five-year Centre-server retention; do not repeat APPEAL-BASE's one-year rule. This does not mean all recordings are publicly accessible. §§36,38 concern reasoned grant/refusal decisions and binding implementation. §§43–44 address repeated decided or withdrawn matters. §47 refers disagreement with the authorised body's decision to the Administrative Procedural and Process Code; retrieve the actual route/time limits before advising a judicial or administrative challenge.

Produce a reviewable appeal draft when requested, with genuine facts only. Do not sign, send or submit without authorization for that action. Do not promise a score change, funding or a favourable ruling.
