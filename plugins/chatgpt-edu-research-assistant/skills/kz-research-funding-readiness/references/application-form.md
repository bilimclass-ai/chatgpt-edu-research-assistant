# Application structure and evidence map

Source: FUND appendix 5, supplied PDF pp.52–61; form notes include amendment 213/2026 effective 12 July 2026. Check the actual competition form/portal and source-map currency flags before a live submission. FUND §§56,58 also refer PCF applications to appendix 5, but the scientific task and approved PCF call must govern route-specific adaptation. Institutional fundamental funding uses its own appendix 8; commercialization uses its own call form.

## Three parts

1. Abstract (аннотация).
2. Explanatory note (пояснительная записка).
3. Requested-funding calculation, tables 2–7 entered in the Centre's system.

## Limits and required content

| Part / explanatory-note section | Stated word limit | Content/evidence to develop |
|---|---:|---|
| Abstract | 600 | Goal, problem, approaches, results, scientific/personnel capacity and practical significance |
| 1.1 Project title | 30 | Accurate topic, consistent across all files/languages |
| 1 General information | No general limit specified here | Priority, specialized direction/type, dates/months, requested total and annual amounts in thousands of KZT, matching keywords |
| 2 Overall concept | 750 | Contains 2.1–2.3; check both total and subsection limits |
| 2.1 Introduction | 150 | Idea and concrete problem |
| 2.2 Goal | 100 | One precise achievable aim aligned with title and main research question |
| 2.3 Tasks | 500 | Connected measurable tasks, contribution to goal, outputs and readiness information |
| 3 Scientific novelty and significance | 2500 | World/Kazakhstan literature, knowledge gap, comparison, prior work, expected contribution and conditions for impact |
| 4 Methods and ethical questions | 2500 | Questions/hypotheses, design, experiments where relevant, data/sources/analysis, reproducibility, ethics and IP allocation/protection |
| 5 Research group and management | No limit specified here | Table 1; core/additional staff, role evidence, PI eligibility publications and core-team publication inventory |
| 6 Research environment | 1000 | Actual facilities, access, trained operators, collaboration, outsourcing and research mobility |
| 7 Budget justification | 1500 | Tables 2–7, quantity/rate calculations, price evidence, necessary costs and exact reconciliation |
| 8 Implementation plan | No limit specified here | Table 8, work sequence, timing, milestones and accountable outputs |
| 9 Expected results | 750 | Measurable main result, quality/quantity, output form, users, application and substantiated effects |
| 10 Bibliography | No limit specified here | Complete references supporting novelty claims; resolve all in-text references |

The source moves its referenced tables, diagrams and charts to an explanatory-note appendix and excludes them from the stated section word/page calculation. Do not move ordinary prose into tables to evade a limit. Check each required language separately and the actual portal's counting rules. An automated whitespace count is a preparation estimate, not a guarantee of the portal's count.

The source novelty section cross-refers to section 8 as bibliography although its actual bibliography is section 10. Preserve the correct structure of the approved call and reconcile this cross-reference openly; do not delete the implementation plan. Do not confuse these applicant limits with GNTE experts' shorter comment limits.

## Table and evidence checklist

| Table | Purpose | Non-obvious check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research team | At least 70% detailed planned staff; up to 30% additional positions with role/work/recruitment approach. At least 10 core-team publications in aggregate, not per member; verify PI requirements separately. |
| 2 | Consolidated requested budget | Total and year totals equal general-information §1.5 and the detailed calculations; units are thousands of KZT where specified. |
| 3 | Labour and mandatory charges | Effort, roles and statutory treatment consistent with work plan; avoid charging the same team member again as an outsourced service. |
| 4 | Travel | Research purpose, travel plan and carrier ticket-price evidence; use applicable applicant travel regulations, not council reimbursement rules. |
| 5 | Scientific/organizational support and other services/works | At least one quote and/or price list as the form requires; specify outsourced provider, work, volume and output. |
| 6 | Materials, equipment/software | Supporting quotes; equipment/software purchases restricted to legal entities in this form. |
| 7 | Rental and equipment/technical operating expenses | Supporting quotes, actual access need and direct project connection. |
| 8 | Implementation schedule | Every task produces an observable output by a realistic date. |
| 9 | Private-partner contribution plan | Applied-research conditions and route/discipline exceptions must be checked before declaring it mandatory. |

Read TRL language as a requirement to evidence the applicable stage, not permission to inflate readiness. Determine the applicable Kazakhstan readiness methodology and call interpretation, particularly for non-technological research. Do not invent a TRL number from a narrative. Publications, prototypes, IP, adoption and sales are different outputs: connect each to verifiable completion evidence and the actual call's minimum results.
