## Known unresolved changes — do not hide these

1. **NNS amendment V2600039378:** https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/V2600039378 . Official indexed title confirms an amendment to Order 487. Full text, enactment date and changed provisions could not be verified despite multiple routes. Reconcile the amendment before asserting current NNS scores, thresholds, ranking, budget-cut rules or procedure. Edition-specific practice may proceed with this limitation stated.
2. **Law 321-VIII of 24 June 2026:** https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/Z2600000321 . The uploaded LAW flags prospective changes, including articles 20, 22, 25 and 36, after sixty days from first official publication. Secondary publisher metadata at https://prg.kz/document/?doc_id=35487579 reports staggered commencement including 25 August 2026, but the relevant full changes were not accessible. Do not assume these provisions remain prospective on 7 September. Obtain the authoritative amendment and publication date before applying affected eligibility/governance provisions as current.

## Refresh procedure

At a new application or appeal, establish jurisdiction, route, event date and call; check canonical amendment history and first publication. Maintain four separate fields: adopted date, first publication, effective date by provision, and retrieval date. Read a replacement provision in full, including its exceptions and language-specific changes. Use the law, implementing orders, approved call and portal instructions in their proper roles; flag conflicts rather than silently rewriting the law. A current check of one document does not establish currency of the others. Refresh the candidate's call and any late amendments too. If inaccessible, use the verified subset, identify affected decisions and request the exact missing text; continue unaffected drafting and arithmetic.
