# Kazakhstan and multilingual evidence

Apply when Kazakhstan, Kazakh/Russian scholarship, a local policy question or the user's database requirement makes this layer relevant. Do not assume every person in the shared university workspace studies Kazakhstan.

Search observed Kazakh Cyrillic and Russian terms plus English equivalents; add transliteration/Latin variants only when useful. Local terminology may differ from direct translation. Distinguish proposed translations from terms confirmed in retrieved literature. Include national journals, university repositories and official sources in separate layers from international research.

Treat ККСОН/ККСОН МОН РК/КОКСНВО and current official names as search aliases, not proof of identical current status. It is a recommended-publication list, not a bibliographic database equivalent to Scopus. Check the official list/order and applicable date, subject series and journal identity when possible. Publisher statements and university slides are weaker corroboration; label their date and level. A 2021 order or 2025 slide does not prove 2026 eligibility. Do not infer index status from a university's reputation.

Separate international, Central Asian/regional, Kazakhstan academic and official/contextual layers. Neighboring-country evidence is not direct Kazakhstan evidence. Applicability ratings are: direct (relevant local data), indirect (an explicit plausible context/mechanism bridge), uncertain (insufficient comparability), not applicable. A local affiliation alone does not make study findings local.

For teaching or institutional research, attend to language, resources, rural/urban settings, institutional type and participant access when supported. Do not claim a program-owner partnership, identifiable data access, ethical approval or implementation capacity is secured. Suggested future studies should use suitable approvals and data governance; a literature review itself does not authorize an intervention.
